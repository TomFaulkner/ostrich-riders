Ostrich Riders for Omarchy
==========================

This plugin is a derivative of Ostrich Riders, a free clone of the
arcade game Joust.

  Original development: Seby <sebygames@gmail.com>
  Original graphics:    Suiland
  Continuation:         Dennis Payne <dulsi@identicalsoftware.com>
  Upstream:             https://github.com/dulsi/ostrichriders

Physics constants, standard maps, sprite sheets, and sound effects
are taken from that project and remain licensed under the GNU General
Public License version 3 or later.

The Omarchy overlay, bar widget, and JavaScript port of the rules were
written for this plugin.
